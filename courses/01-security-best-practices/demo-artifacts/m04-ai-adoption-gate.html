Verdict · dashed border

Marketing chatbot: shadow AI

Never entered the gate. Discovery found it; governance never saw it. Blocking it at the edge moves the same behavior onto phones, so the answer is a sanctioned path plus a policy, not a firewall rule.

Verdict · solid border

Order-intake pilot: blocked at gate

Two of five decisions recorded. Better than shadow AI and still not shippable. The data boundary and the risk owner are the two that stop it, and neither is a technical problem.

The recommendation

Define AI security objectives and governance policy, then baseline. Both workloads fail on the same two fields, and no content filter, edge block, or detection rule fills either one. Until a named person owns AI risk and the data boundary is written down, every control deployed is enforcing a rule that does not exist yet.
