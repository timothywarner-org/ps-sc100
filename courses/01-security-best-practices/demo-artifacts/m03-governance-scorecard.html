Architectural finding

The subscription problem was a platform gap, not a process failure. Globomantics was not missing a feature; it was missing a decision and an inheritance path to carry it. Policy assigned at the management group now reaches subscriptions that do not exist yet. The remaining strategy gaps are decisions, not controls, and no amount of tooling closes them.
