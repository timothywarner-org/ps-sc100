Read with these limits

CAE is bounded; password changes can be critical events

Conditional Access also evaluates token refresh. CAE depends on supported resource, client, user and event combinations, and critical events can take up to 15 minutes to propagate. Password change/reset is a CAE critical event, so you can't say it does nothing to an issued access token. HR termination must drive a supported identity action.

One excluded group is not proof of an estate-wide bypass

Report-only evaluates without enforcing that policy. Inspect every applicable policy and actual sign-in results. The demo estate also contains CA-01 targeting all users, so CA-04 exclusions alone do not establish effective MFA coverage.

Certificate renewal is not automatic consumer repair

A certificate object has linked key and secret objects. Automatic renewal depends on issuer integration and policy, and each consumer must adopt the new version. A secret can have expiry metadata. Keeping cryptographic operations in the vault requires a compatible use case; not every TLS workload can keep its private key there.

Cloud emergency access and forest recovery are different

Independent cloud-only emergency identities preserve tenant administration. They do not restore an AD DS forest. Design and test the two recovery paths separately; monitoring is one safeguard, not what makes an exclusion safe by itself.

Plane separation is an architectural control, not a product rule that no account can ever span planes. Routine eligible access has deliberate emergency exceptions. Unused grants can support infrequent work, so removal can cause an outage. Reviews are not inherently samples, and access packages do not prevent all grants made elsewhere.
